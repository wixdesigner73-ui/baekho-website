FAQ

Questions parents ask us

Will my child enjoy it?

Our own syllabus uses story-telling and gamification, so lessons feel like an adventure. The easiest way to find out is a trial class.

Is it safe?

Little Taeks classes are small groups, so every child feels safe and secure, and our coaches are experienced and certified. Safety policies, equipment and supervision details: to be confirmed by Baekho

Will a beginner fit in?

Classes are grouped by age (3–6, 7–12 and 13–17), and our child-led approach meets each student where they are. Details on levels and mixed-ability groups: to be confirmed by Baekho

Are the coaches qualified?

Baekho’s coaches are experienced, certified masters and championship-winning international coaches. Individual coach profiles are on the About page.

Is it worth the fee?

Taekwondo memberships include weekly training, an exclusive training app, the Baekho Compass syllabus, monthly progress reports and access to all locations. Prices are on the Locations page. prices to be confirmed

What happens at the first class?

Your child will meet the coach and get a feel for the class. A step-by-step first-class guide, including what to wear and bring: to be supplied by Baekho

Can we train at both locations?

Taekwondo memberships list unlimited access to all locations. Other programs run at specific dojos: see each program for where. confirm still current

How do payments and cancellation work?

Memberships are paid by auto-debit or credit. The Basic Taekwondo plan lists “cancel anytime”, and a 12-month rate lock-in is also listed. confirm terms

How do I get in touch?

Message us on WhatsApp at +6012-513-8813, or email admin@baekho.my. We’re happy to help you pick a class.
