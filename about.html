About Baekho

More than punch and kick

At Baekho, martial arts is about growing the whole child: values, a sense of self, and awareness of the people around them. Serious coaching and high standards, delivered through fun.

Respect All. Fear None.
Our story

An academy that grew up with its students

Baekho is now a multi-location academy in the Klang Valley with more programs, an evolved teaching methodology, and learning resources for students and parents.

Our child-led principles and methods of training focus on components beyond physical skill. We emphasise values, growing a sense of self, and awareness for those around us. Mind, heart and body, together, grow your child into a whole and well-balanced individual.

What we believe

Our guiding thought

“Learning is never cumulative. It is a movement of knowing, which has no beginning and no end.”

Respect

Courtesy and integrity: for coaches, classmates and everyone around us.

Perseverance

Keep going, learn from mistakes, and try again.

Self-control

Strength with a calm head: the tenet of every taekwondo student.

Fear None

Confidence to try new things, without ever being intimidating.

The Baekho methodology

Redefining martial arts

Martial arts have been taught the same way for centuries. Our focus is to make training accessible to everyone, and its benefits available to anyone.
